The offer is intended for traders interested in joining, but actual eligibility may depend on account type, location, instrument and other program conditions. Contact the Telegram admin to verify who qualifies.

When and how will rebates be paid?

Payment frequency, qualification rules, available trading accounts and any exclusions must be confirmed with the Telegram admin before participation.

Does the calculator show guaranteed earnings?

No. It estimates a potential rebate based on entered eligible lots. Rebates are separate from trading profits or losses. Leveraged trading can lead to substantial financial loss.
